    How plans are labeled, and what to keep in mind
    • Dark Age rush: 500+ resources of army made before reaching Feudal (about 5 units). Checked first, so a rusher is never labeled anything else. About 5% of players overall; mostly Mongols and Vikings.
    • Fast Castle: Castle Age by 8:00, or by 10:00 with little army at 8:00.
    • Feudal all-in: army value at 8:00 in the top 20% for that civ, team size and rank, and few villagers made between 5:00 and 8:00. No extra town center by 8:00.
    • Feudal pressure: army value at 8:00 in the top 40%.
    • Boom: villagers by 8:00 in the top 30% (or an extra town center by 8:00) with little army at 8:00.
    • Balanced: none of the above. Games that ended before 10:00 are not labeled.
    • Plans are read from the first 8 minutes, what the player set out to do (since 2026-11-24). Reading to 15:00 partly recorded how the game went: a boomer who got pressured stopped making villagers and was called Balanced, which made Boom and Feudal pressure look about 1–3 points better and Balanced about 2 points worse than they were.
    • Percentiles are taken within the same civ, team size and rank bracket, so a civ's normal play (French knights, Jin mounted villagers) does not decide the label.
    • "Expected" comes from the MMR difference between the two teams, fitted on all games. A plan that only stronger players use does not get credit for their skill.
    • Army and villager counts at 8:00 can still reflect the first fights a little (a Dark Age rush or an early Feudal raid), and Fast Castle by 10:00 needs the player to get there. Balanced players tend to be those who neither built army nor boomed well, which is partly a skill signal within a rank.
    • Trade and late-game habits show up mostly in long games that a team is already winning. Read the trade tag as a sign of a good position, not a cause.
    • Elo tiers: a game's tier is the average matchmaking Elo of all its players for that team size (the per-mode Elo AoE4 World shows, not the Diamond/Conqueror badge points, which are shared across team sizes). Elo runs about 70 points above badge points for a typical team.
    • Games with an AFK or early-leaving player are excluded. Ranked games only, Season 14 only. The 95% ranges treat each game as one observation.
    • Forum advice: each claim is tested on one row of our tables. Supported means the significant result has the sign the advice predicts; contradicted means the opposite sign; no reliable effect means there is no significant row.
    • Newest games: lineup, pair and build-order results are also found from the older games alone, then checked on the newest two weeks, which played no part in finding them. ✓ held up = the newest games point the same way; ✗ didn't hold = they point the other way (left out of the lineups); new = the older games alone were not enough, so the newest games are part of the evidence. Shown for the patch 17 and all-patches views.
    • Current patch pools every build since the last major patch (now 17.0, builds 12182 on): the current balance. It is the default view. "All Season 14 patches" is mostly older patch 16 games.
    • Trends: pick share is a count, not a test; only the coloured vs ratings numbers are tested results.
    • Significance: every result is tested against zero (no effect), and the p-values are corrected for the many results in each chart and view (Benjamini–Hochberg). Only results that stay significant at a 5% false-discovery rate are shown; everything else is left out, so an empty chart or blank cell means no reliable effect, not a zero.
